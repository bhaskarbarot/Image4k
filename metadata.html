Good to know

What metadata is in my photos?

Most photos embed EXIF data: GPS coordinates of where it was taken, the camera/phone model, the date and time, and editing software. Sharing a photo online can therefore reveal your home or location.

Does removing it change my photo's quality?

No. JPG and PNG are cleaned losslessly — the pixels are untouched, only the hidden data blocks are removed. Other formats (WebP etc.) are re-encoded at high quality.

Is it private?

Completely. Everything happens in your browser — your photos are never uploaded to any server.

Why does the file get a little smaller?

Because the metadata (and sometimes an embedded thumbnail) is removed. The visible image is unchanged.
