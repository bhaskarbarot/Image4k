    How temp mail works
    
      
    Does it really receive email and OTP codes?
    Yes. The address is a real mailbox on our server. Any email sent to it — including sign-up confirmations and one-time passwords — arrives here within a few seconds, and we automatically highlight the verification code.
    
      
    Is it private?
    The inbox can only be read with a secret key stored in your browser, not by anyone who merely knows the address. Everything auto-deletes when the address expires (about an hour of inactivity, 24 hours at most). Don't send anything sensitive or important — it's disposable by design.
    
      
    Can I keep the same address?
    Your address stays in this browser until it expires or you pick a new one with “Change” or “Delete”. Reopening this page keeps the same inbox.
    
      
    Why shouldn't I use it for real accounts?
    Because it's temporary: once it expires you lose access, and you could never recover a real account tied to it. Use it for throwaway sign-ups, testing and avoiding spam.
