    Good to know
    
        
    Will my QR code always work?
    Each code is decoded right after you change it (at a large and a small size) and you'll see “Scan verified” only when it decodes to exactly your content. Keep dark colours on a light background and leave the quiet zone around it.
    
        
    Do the codes expire?
    No. These are static QR codes — the content is stored inside the image itself, so they work forever and nothing is tracked.
    
        
    Is my data uploaded?
    No. The QR code, your logo and your Wi-Fi password are processed entirely in your browser.
    
        
    Which size should I print?
    At least 2–3 cm (about 1 inch) wide for short links, larger for long content like contacts. For print, download SVG or 2048 px PNG.
