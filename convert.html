Good to know

What is HEIC and why won't it open?

HEIC/HEIF is the format iPhones save photos in. It's efficient but many apps and Windows can't open it. Converting to JPG (or PNG/WebP) makes the photo work everywhere.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser using WebAssembly — your images never leave your device, so it's completely private.

Which should I choose — JPG, PNG or WebP?

JPG for photos you'll share or print (smallest, universal). PNG when you need lossless quality or transparency. WebP for the web — small files at high quality.

Can I convert many at once?

Yes — drop in up to 40 images and grab them all in one ZIP with “Download all”.
